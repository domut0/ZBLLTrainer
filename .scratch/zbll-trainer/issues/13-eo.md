# 13 — EO

Status: ready-for-agent

## What to build

Eleven cases, and a diagram problem that no earlier issue has faced.

`data/source/apb/eopair-dbr.csv` holds EO with the pair already solved into the
**back-right** slot — the current standard, per the sheet's own preamble. Row 1
is a long prose preamble about EO-pair conventions; skip it.

Two header blocks, at CSV lines 6 and 13. The column titles are the **sets of
misoriented edges**: `UF/UR`, `UF/UB`, `UF/FR`, `UF/DR`, `FR/DR`,
`UF/UL/UB/UR`, then `UB/UR/UF/FR`, `UB/UR/UF/DR`, `UF/UR/FR/DR`, `UL/UR/FR/DR`,
`UF/UL/UB/UR/FR/DR`. Six plus five is eleven cases — that is the count check.

Two parsing traps. One cell carries a parenthetical note, `(for misoriented DR)`,
which is not part of the algorithm; strip trailing prose. And like `lxs-fur.csv`,
there is a stray algorithm far below the real data — at line 972 — so bound the
parse to the populated region.

**The diagram is the real work.** EO must show edge *orientation*, and
orientation is not a colour: an oriented and a misoriented edge can present the
same sticker on top. The stage-aware diagram from Issue 12 gets you the geometry
but not this. Something explicit is needed — an arrow, a two-tone marker, a
distinct outline — and whatever it is, it must be derived from the cube state
rather than inferred from sticker colour.

Confirm against the source sheet that the eleven parsed cases are the eleven the
column headers name. Their edge sets are written down, so this is checkable
rather than a matter of trust.
